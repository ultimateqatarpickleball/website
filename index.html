About Qatar Ultimate

Play. Compete. Connect. — building a stronger pickleball community in Doha.

Our story

Bringing Doha together, one game at a time.

Qatar Ultimate is an independent community pickleball club created to make it easier for people in Doha to discover the sport, meet other players and enjoy regular games. Whether you're picking up a paddle for the first time or chasing your next competitive milestone, there's a place for you here.

Our mission

Create an inclusive, energetic and well-organised pickleball community with accessible play, friendly competition and opportunities to grow.

Our values

Everyone belongs on court.

🤝 Community — welcoming players and building friendships.

🎯 Improvement — supporting learning and progression.

🏅 Sportsmanship — competing with respect and fairness.

🇶🇦 Local pride — growing pickleball in Qatar.
